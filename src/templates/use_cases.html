Who actually uses this?

Three workflows where knowing the BTS within 1 km of a coordinate moves the needle.

{# ── Use case 1 ────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
🛰

RF engineer planning a drive-test campaign

Persona: senior radio engineer at a Polish operator or independent network-quality consultancy.

Pain: Drive-test logs are expensive to capture (vehicle + tester + ScanScript license). You only want to drive corridors where the BTS layout actually overlaps — wasting two days on a stretch of highway with no nearby cells is a fired-from-the-account event.

How Signal-Scout helps: feed the route's GPS waypoints (one per km) into POST /stations with max_distance=2 km; the response gives you the operator + band mix expected at every point. Filter for the one you're auditing, export to CSV, plan the actual drive-test only over corridors with ≥2 cells in range. Result: 30–40% fewer windshield-hours per campaign.

curl -H "X-API-Key: $SS_KEY" \
  "https://signal-scout.com/stations?lat=52.23&lng=21.00&max_distance=2&service_provider=Plus"

Plan match: Starter for solo consultants, Pro for the team's shared planning pipeline.

{# ── Use case 2 ────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
📡

Telco infrastructure manager tracking coverage gaps

Persona: head of access networks at a Tier-2 operator or MVNO with leased capacity.

Pain: You sub-lease capacity from one of the big 4 PL operators. When their parent rebalances cells (decommissioned 3G, new 5G site, refarmed band), your customers in the affected postcodes start churning — and you find out from the support queue, two months too late.

How Signal-Scout helps: save your top 50 customer-density spots as alerting locations. Every UKE refresh (~monthly) you get an email per spot where coverage materially changed: a band gained, a band lost, the nearest BTS distance moved more than 1 km. Combined with your churn data, you get 2-month early warning on capacity issues — enough lead time to negotiate a roaming clause or rebrand the affected SKU.

POST /account/locations
{ "name": "Warsaw Mokotów cluster", "lat": 52.18, "lng": 21.02, "alerting_enabled": true }

Plan match: Pro (50+ saved locations, webhooks for ingestion into your ops dashboard).

{# ── Use case 3 ────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
🏢

Property developer scoring buildings on cellular quality

Persona: development director at a residential or office REIT, or a relocation consultancy shortlisting offices for corporate clients.

Pain: "Strong 5G coverage" is a real selling feature for premium offices (think trader floors, indie podcast studios, telehealth clinics). But you don't want to install DAS in every building — you need to know upfront which buildings already have a 5G3600 cell within 500 m before signing a lease.

How Signal-Scout helps: bulk-lookup the lat/lng of 200 candidate addresses via POST /stations in a notebook; rank by closest 5G band distance, by operator count (4 = best), by total bands within 500 m. Output a coverage score column in your shortlist spreadsheet — cuts the building shortlist by 60% before site visits.

# Python — score 200 candidate addresses
for addr in shortlist:
  r = requests.get(API + "/stations",
    params={"lat": addr.lat, "lng": addr.lng, "max_distance": 0.5},
    headers={"X-API-Key": KEY})
  score = sum("5G" in s["frequency_bands"][0] for s in r.json()["stations"])

Plan match: Starter for one-off batches, Pro for ongoing portfolio scoring.
